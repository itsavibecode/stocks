Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026
Stockfolio is a free, independent personal-portfolio tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have. The short version: you can use Stockfolio entirely in your browser without an account, we don't run ads or trackers, and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

Stockfolio ("the app," "we," "us") is a non-commercial hobby project — a single-page web app for tracking and visualizing a personal investment portfolio and its dividends. It is operated by an individual, not a company, and is hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages.

2. The short version

  • Local-only by default. If you don't sign in, everything you enter stays in your own browser. We never receive it.
  • Sign-in is optional. Signing in with Google lets your portfolio sync across your devices. Your data is stored under your own account and is not visible to other users.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking pixels. We don't profile you or sell/share your data for marketing.
  • You bring your own API keys for market data, and you can delete your data at any time.

3. Information we collect

a) When you use Stockfolio without signing in

Your portfolio (tickers, share lots, entry prices, realized sales, preferences, activity log, and cached prices/news) is saved only in your browser's localStorage. It never leaves your device and we have no access to it.

b) When you sign in with Google

We use Firebase Authentication (a Google service). When you sign in, we receive your Google account's email address, a unique user ID, and basic profile info (display name and avatar) to identify your account. Your portfolio data is then stored in Google Firestore under your user ID so it can sync across devices. This includes the tickers you track, your share lots and cost basis, realized-sale records, your settings/preferences, an in-app activity log, and cached price/news data.

c) Email address (for optional notifications)

If you enable dividend reminders or the monthly digest, we use the email address tied to your Google sign-in to send those emails. You can turn these off in Settings at any time.

d) API keys you provide

To fetch live prices and news, you supply your own free API keys (Finnhub, Alpha Vantage, Tiingo, Polygon, or Twelve Data). These are stored with the rest of your preferences (locally, and in Firestore if you're signed in) and are used only to make requests to those providers on your behalf.

4. How your information is used

  • To store and sync your portfolio across your devices.
  • To display prices, news, dividend dates, and the charts/estimates that are the point of the app.
  • To send the reminder and digest emails you opt into.
  • To connect to your brokerage in read-only mode if you choose to use the optional SnapTrade sync.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the features you use.

5. Third-party services

Stockfolio relies on the following third parties. Your use of the app is also subject to their privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore) — sign-in and cloud storage of your portfolio.
  • Market-data APIs you configure (Finnhub, Alpha Vantage, Tiingo, Polygon, Twelve Data) — requests go directly from your browser to fetch quotes and news; the ticker symbols you track are included in those requests.
  • CoinGecko — Bitcoin price for the market ticker (no personal data sent).
  • logo.dev — company and brokerage logos; ticker/brokerage identifiers appear in image URLs.
  • SnapTrade (optional) — read-only brokerage connection. You authorize it through SnapTrade's own flow; we store a secret token to fetch your positions and transactions. We never see or store your brokerage username or password.
  • Resend — delivers the reminder/digest emails (receives your email address and the email content).
  • Cloudflare Workers — our lightweight backend proxy that talks to SnapTrade and sends emails, so credentials never reach your browser.
  • GitHub Pages — static site hosting; GitHub may log standard request metadata (such as IP address and browser type).
  • Google Fonts & Cloudflare CDN — serve fonts and one image-export script; these may see your IP address as part of normal delivery.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't show ads or embed advertising trackers.
  • We don't run analytics or behavioral tracking.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your personal data for marketing.
  • We don't access your brokerage login, and SnapTrade access is read-only — Stockfolio cannot place trades or move money.

7. Cookies & local storage

Stockfolio does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Firebase Authentication stores a login token in your browser (via local storage / IndexedDB) to keep you signed in. The app uses localStorage to save your portfolio and preferences on your device. Clearing your browser storage removes this local data.

8. Data retention & your choices

  • Local data: stays until you clear your browser storage or use the app's reset option.
  • Cloud data: stored in Firestore until you delete it. The app includes an option to delete your cloud portfolio document; doing so removes it from Firestore.
  • Emails: turn off reminders and the monthly digest in Settings to stop receiving them.
  • Sign out at any time to stop syncing to the cloud.

9. Data security

All traffic to and from the app is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Firestore security rules restrict each user's portfolio document so it can only be read or written by that signed-in user. The shared caches the app maintains (for company fundamentals and benchmark prices) contain only public market data — never your holdings or personal information. That said, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and you use the app at your own risk.

10. Children's privacy

Stockfolio is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. International users

The services Stockfolio relies on (Google, Cloudflare, and others) may process and store data in the United States and elsewhere. By using the app you understand your data may be handled in those locations.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

13. Contact

Stockfolio is an independent, non-commercial project. For privacy questions, or to request deletion of data we hold, you can open an issue on the project's public repository (github.com/itsavibecode/stocks) or use the account/data-deletion options inside the app.
